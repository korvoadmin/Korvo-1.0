# Korvo-1.0
Korvo 1.0 connects customers and businesses with independent professionals for legitimate project-based services and qualifying gigs.

The broader research vision includes projects, shifts and everyday gigs. Employee staffing, hotel/restaurant employee-shift placement, payroll and agency/EOR integrations are deferred to a potential Korvo 2.0. Existing research and disabled code are preserved in [the staffing archive](docs/KORVO_2_0_STAFFING_ARCHIVE.md).

First beta: invitation-only, 10 customers + 10 professionals in metro Atlanta, approved lower-risk project categories and no real money.

Launch status and remaining work: [Master launch checklist](docs/KORVO_MASTER_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md).

Public shift bookings, employee payroll routes, live payments and contractor transfers remain disabled pending the documented engineering, business and approval gates.
